# Mute sounds

Silence exactly the sounds you don't want to hear, and keep the rest.

Open the mod's config in the app and tick what to mute:

- **Music, menus, enemies & more**: every sound, all music, voices, menu
  clicks, ambience, a boss, a whole enemy family, and so on.
- **One list per hero** (Scarlet, Piper, Beowulf, Snow Queen, Aladdin,
  Melusine, Geppetto, Sun Wukong, Carmilla, Romeo, Juliet, Merlin, Kintaro):
  every sound that hero makes, one by one, grouped by folder (abilities,
  skills, barks, footsteps…), plus "everything in this list".

The lists come straight from your installed game's sound bank, so they always
match your game version. Untick a sound and it comes back. Nothing in the game
files is changed.

Only affects your own speakers: other players in co-op still hear everything.

Needs loader v51 or newer (`rsmm update-loader`, or update the app).
